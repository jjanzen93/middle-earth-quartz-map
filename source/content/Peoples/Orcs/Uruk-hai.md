The Uruk-hai are a breed of [[Orcs|Orc]], basically manufactured by Saruman in Isengard. They are more powerful than standard Orcs, as the sunlight does not weaken them and they can run for days without tiring. They're also taller and stronger with darker skin.

Uruk-hai tend to have more standardized weaponry and armor as provided by Saruman, with the White Hand of Isengard emblazoned on their shields. All of these factors mean they think of themselves very highly, like an elite fighting force that is far superior to their fellow Orc soldiers. Some might say this is a weakness, as it makes infighting far more likely as they go out of their way to boast and tease weaker Orcs.

**Citations**: _The Two Towers_, Book 3, Ch. 3: The Uruk-hai (p. 444-460).
