People
The Men of Rohan are from Rohan (duh), a kingdom near Gondor, with its ruler residing in Edoras. They are good friends of the [[Men of Gondor]] after their land was gifted to them in [[The Battle of the Field of Celebrant]].

They are proud, bold, and generous, much like those from Gondor, though their courtesy towards outsiders had dwindled while Grima Wormtongue had been poisoning the mind of King Theoden.

Their society revolves around their mastery of horsemanship, both in riding, battle, and husbandry. Specifically, they are capable of breeding powerful horses known as the *Mearas* that, in addition to extreme physical capabilities, can understand the speech of Men. These horses usually can only be ridden by the King or his sons, with the exception of Gandalf and Shadowfax.
