People
The Men of Gondor are the inhabitants of the surviving Southern Kingdom of the Númenóreans. They were heavily involved in [[The Last Alliance of Elves and Men]], and now hold the line at the border of Mordor. Additionally, they were saved by the [[Men of Rohan]] in [[The Battle of the Field of Celebrant]], which led to the gifting of what is now Rohan.

Men of Gondor tend to be extremely proud and think of themselves as very disciplined. They are great builders, like with stone cities and fortresses like Minas Tirith and Osgiliath. In the Fellowship, Boromir is their representative, as he and his brother had dreams about the events of the War of the Ring. Like many Men, they desire redemption once they have taken steps down the wrong path, as was the case when Boromir was slain defending Merry and Pippin despite his weakness to the Ring's temptation.

In Boromir's death, we can also catch a glimpse at their funeral rights, with Boromir sent down the river with his own weapons, that of his slain enemies, and his Great Horn, an important artifact to Gondor.
