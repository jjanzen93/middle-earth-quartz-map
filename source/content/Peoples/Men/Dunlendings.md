People
The Dunlendings, or the Wild Men, live in the hills of Dunland west of Rohan. They have a longstanding hatred of the [[Men of Rohan]] after what they believed were their rightful lands in Calenardhon were gifted to them after [[The Battle of the Field of Celebrant]].

They are tall and dark haired, and they speak their own language which is described as harsh and guttural (similar to Orcs and the Black Speech), and usually do not speak Westron. This is indicative of a deep isolation from the other peoples of Middle-earth, a trait that was not overlooked by Saruman. In addition to feeding off their desire for revenge, he used the fact that few (if any) living Dunlendings knew anything about the Men of Rohan to convince them that they were violent and cruel. He then took this anger, now whipped into a frenzy, armed the Dunlendings, and pointed them at Rohan.
