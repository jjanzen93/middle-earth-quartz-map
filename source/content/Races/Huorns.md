Race
Huorns are shrouded in mystery in the story. It is unclear from descriptions of them if they are [[Ents]] that have become tree-like or trees that have become Ent-like. In either case, the two are closely related, as Huorns tend to be herded by Ents much like normal trees.

They are incredibly fast, and create a heavy darkness wherever they travel. Although they are sentient, they cannot communicate with any being other than Ents, and tend to be closer to wild animals than people. Despite this, they have a deep sense of revenge, and hate [[Orcs]] and axes for the damage they have wrought on the forests.

As a result, Huorns are very violent, tearing apart anyone who they deem deserves it. Their bodies are destroyed to such a degree that very little is left once they are finished. This is evidenced by the vanishing of the retreating Orcs at [[The Battle of Helm's Deep]]. It seems they never heard the Age-old saying: Mess with the forest, you get the Huorns.

**Citations:** _The Two Towers_, Book 3, Ch. 9: Flotsam and Jetsam (p. 565-571).
