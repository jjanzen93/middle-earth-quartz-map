Race
Ents are tall tree-like creatures with skin made of bark and deep, mystical green eyes. They are essentially tree shepherds, keeping forests safe from the denizens of Middle-earth that would look to harm them. They tend to be very slow and methodical in everything they do, from decision-making to conversation, as seen in their councils called Entmoots. In these meetings, this methodical nature is represented in their language, as anything spoken in Entish takes a very long time to say. Despite this, they are immensely powerful, strong, and quick when they want to be.

Their strength was put on full display at [[The Destruction of Isengard]], where they ripped all but Orthanc itself to shreds. This event also proved their intelligence and patience, hiding around the outskirts of Isengard instead of continuing their rampage so that they might catch Saruman if he attempted to flee.

Ents are a dwindling race, as the Entwives disappeared long ago and thus no children, or Entings, have been sired. This is the Ents' greatest sorrow, and is the main reason that more and more Ents stop moving and speaking, becoming tree-like. It is unclear if these tree-like Ents are [[Huorns]], or if they are two separate types of being.
