Race
Orcs are corrupted beings that were essentially bred by the Darkness in an attempt to imitate the Elves in the First Age. They are weakened by the Sun and move primarily at night or underground, as evidenced by their presence in [[Balin's Expedition|Moria]] (Saruman's [[Uruk-hai]] are the exception to this). They are also incapable of creating anything beautiful, and are instead brutal weaponsmiths and torturers. 

Much like the Minions from *Despicable Me*, Orcs require one evil and powerful ruler to actually get anything done, as they often fall to grudges and infighting. Even when they are appropriately led, these problems tend to be their downfall, as evidenced by the fate of the Orc troop that kidnapped Merry and Pippin.

**Citations:** _The Fellowship of the Ring_, Book 2, Ch. 5: The Bridge of Khazad-dûm (p. 320-323); _The Two Towers_, Book 3, Ch. 3: The Uruk-hai (p. 444-460).
