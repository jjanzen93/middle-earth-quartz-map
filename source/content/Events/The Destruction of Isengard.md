Event
After an Entmoot, Treebeard led the [[Ents]] and [[Huorns]] to Isengard, as Saruman's betrayal had become known and could no longer be ignored as more and more trees perished at the [[Uruk-hai]]'s hand. Saruman had sent most of his forces away to Helm's Deep, and after a short period of waiting for them all to file out of the fortress, the Ents easily broke through the walls of Isengard like they were nothing.

The fires of Isengard drove them further into an unstoppable frenzy, hurting themselves against the magically impenetrable stone walls of Orthanc. In a moment of clarity, Treebeard recognizes the danger their desire for revenge posed to themselves, and instead instructs the Ents to use the river Isen to flood all the underground forges, then lie in wait, watching to see if Saruman would attempt to flee.

Here we once again see revenge act as the downfall of a people. For the [[Dunlendings]] in [[The Battle of Helm's Deep]], their desire for revenge against the [[Men of Rohan]] could have led to their death (many did die during the battle) if it weren't for the mercy granted by King Theoden. For the Ents, Saruman might have escaped through the self-harming carnage of the attack on Orthanc if it weren't for his own shrill laugh and Treebeard's cautioning.
