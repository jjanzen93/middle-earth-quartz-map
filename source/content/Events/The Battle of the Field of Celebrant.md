Event
The Battle of the Celebrant caused the founding of Rohan after the Rohirrim (now the [[Men of Rohan]]), led by Eorl the Young, rode from the North to [[Men of Gondor|Gondor]]'s aid hundreds of years ago. Gondor was under threat of annihilation from an invasion to the East when a great number of calvalry came down and drove the enemy out of Gondor. As a result, the Steward of Gondor gifted a province to the Rohirrim, leading to the creation of the Kingdom of Rohan.

The province was depopulated because of the war, but its original inhabitants were the [[Dunlendings]]. After the fight was over, they attempted to repopulate the region, but were rebuked by its new inhabitants. This began the longstanding feud used by Saruman to get their allegiance against Rohan in [[The Battle of Helm's Deep]].

**Citations:** _The Two Towers_, Book 3, Ch. 6: The King of the Golden Hall (p. 512-513).
