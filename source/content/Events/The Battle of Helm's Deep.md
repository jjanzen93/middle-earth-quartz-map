Event
The entirety of [[Men of Rohan|Rohan]]'s fighting force as well as its civilians fled to Helm's Deep upon learning of Saruman's invasion. Hoping that they'd be weakened by their isolation and by Grima Wormtongue's poisoning of King Theoden's mind, Saruman sent thousands of [[Orcs]] and [[Dunlendings]] to put an end to Rohan.

At first, the battle was slow, but after the attackers used an explosive (the "fire of Orthanc"), the Deeping Wall had been breached and the outlook was not good for those inside Helm's Deep. At dawn, however, Gandalf arrived with Erkenbrand, the lord of the Westfold, and a thousand soldiers behind them, and the tide of the battle shifted.

As the Orcs attempted to flee, they found themselves faced with a forest that wasn't there before. The [[Huorns]], sent by Treebeard after meeting with Gandalf post-[[The Destruction of Isengard|Battle of Isengard]], destroyed the fleeing forces, leaving no bodies behind. The Dunlendings had not followed the Orcs, and were shocked when King Theoden let them freely return to their lands instead of delivering cruel punishment, like burning them alive or forcing them to listen to the [*Sweet Caroline (Diagnostix Bootleg)* remix](https://youtu.be/sxcBSkblsn8?si=tbZcgRT1rqyJsX_i&t=32) for an hour. In his attempt to drive them apart, Saruman had inadvertently brought Rohan and Dunland to a more understanding place than they had been for hundreds of years.

**Citations:** _The Two Towers_, Book 3, Ch. 7: Helm's Deep (p. 526-542), Ch. 8: The Road to Isengard (p. 545), Ch. 9: Flotsam and Jetsam (p. 570).
